M.S. Graduation Thesis

Systematic Evaluation of 168 SSL–MIL Combinations for Oncotype DX Prediction

First AuthorSSL · MILDigital PathologyAccepted (Thesis)
문제
Oncotype DX 검사는 비싸고 접근성이 낮아, 병리 이미지(WSI)로 재발 위험을 예측하려는 시도가 많습니다. 하지만 어떤 특징 추출기와 MIL 모델 조합이 다른 기관 데이터에서도 통하는지는 체계적으로 비교된 적이 없었습니다.
내 역할
단독 연구(석사 학위논문). 데이터 정리와 WSI 전처리, 공개된 SSL·MIL 코드를 하나로 통합한 168개 조합 평가 파이프라인 구축, 실험 설계, 통계 분석, 논문 작성을 맡았습니다.
핵심 결과
외부 검증에서는 MIL 구조보다 특징 추출기 선택이 성능을 더 크게 좌우했습니다. 내부 1위였던 MIL(MeanMIL)이 외부에서는 11위로 떨어졌고, 3-class 예측에서 Low-risk 구분은 여전히 어려웠습니다.
기간2025.10 – 2026.07상태Accepted, M.S. Thesis

What problem did I solve?

1. Background

Oncotype DX is expensive and not easily accessible, making treatment decisions difficult in many clinical settings.

Research Questions

  • Which is more important: Feature Extractor or MIL?
  • Does SSL consistently improve performance?
  • Which architecture generalizes best across institutions?

Objective

Systematically evaluate SSL and MIL combinations for robust and generalizable WSI-based Oncotype DX prediction.

What were the results?

4. Results

  • Finding 1
    : Feature Extractor selection had a larger impact than MIL architecture under external validation.
  • Finding 2
    : Attention-based MIL improved cross-institution generalization.
  • Finding 3
    : SSL did not consistently outperform supervised models across all cohorts.
  • Finding 4
    : Low-risk class remained difficult in 3-class prediction.

What did I learn?

Technical Insight

  • Model architecture alone does not guarantee generalization.
  • Representation learning plays a larger role than expected.

Research Insight

  • External validation changes the ranking of models.
  • Evaluation protocol is as important as model development.
